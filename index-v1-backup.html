Agrocom — Redesign Preview

The farmers' super-app on one disciplined system: one green (#16A34A) instead of five, ClashDisplay kept as the display voice with Inter for body text (today a single Medium weight is synthesized into every bold), real ₦ symbols instead of ASCII "N", lucide iconography replacing emoji, and zero picsum.photos placeholders — every image slot gets a designed fallback.

green scale · earth (produce/market) · sky (weather) · gold (ratings) · semantic red
Display / ClashDisplay — headlines, prices, buttons
Body / Inter 500–600 — posts, rows, forms
Caption / Inter 400 — timestamps, helper text
Two-font system: brand voice on display, a real text face for reading.

Photography-led onboarding (the farm imagery is the brand) with the language picker respected — and the app actually translated after it (today only 7 files use i18n across 20 shipped locales).
