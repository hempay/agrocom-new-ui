Synthesis — “The Market Square” design language

The lane: declarative, sunlight-first utility

Every study split the world the same way: entertainment apps spend craft on immersion (dark chrome, gradients, content bleeding into UI); the best utility/rural apps spend it on declaration — every surface states its type, place, price and next action in the first glance. Agrocom's craft budget goes to: ClashDisplay headlines and ₦ figures at display size, one confident green, honest in-situ photography, chunky word-labeled controls, and flat high-contrast surfaces that survive sun glare and dusty thumbs. This is the light board of the four — the opposite pole from Plemuz's black stage.

Non-negotiable rules (apply to every screen)

  • One green. #16A34A for action, links, verified, success — the five circulating greens collapse to it. Gold #D9A521 is strictly merit and money-advantage: best answer, trusted trader, savings, freshness. Sky-blue belongs to weather alone.
  • Big honest numbers: every ₦ amount, ETA and temperature in ClashDisplay at display size; Inter tabular for ledger rows. Always the real ₦ glyph (U+20A6) with thousands separators — fix formatPrice.ts.
  • Words over icons wherever there are consequences: labeled actions ("Like · 5 comments"), state chips in words ("In escrow", "Pending", "Negotiable"), the consequence in the button ("Buy ₦51,000", "Send ₦22,500 offer").
  • Sunlight contrast floor: body text ≥16px near-black on light surfaces, metadata never lighter than grey-600, ≥7:1 for meta, ≥48px targets, solid scrims under any text on photos.
  • Photography is evidence, not aesthetic: unfiltered 4:3 in-situ produce shots — heaps, sacks, hands holding the yam. No stock, no picsum (24 production fallbacks die), no dark gradient glamour.
  • Place is identity: place-first context lines everywhere ("Adamu · Kano · Tomato Growers · 2h"), distance chips, region switcher in the feed header, farm-plot-pinned forecasts.
  • ClashDisplay in real weights — stop synthesizing bold from ClashDisplay-Medium; load the proper weights alongside Inter.

Signature elements (what makes Agrocom unmistakably Agrocom)

  • The unit-price lockup — ₦ figure in ClashDisplay with a unit suffix chip ("₦45,000 / bag") used identically on listing cards, product pages, offer bubbles and receipts. No other board prices per bag.
  • Structured offer bubbles — "Offered ₦45,000/bag × 10 · Accept / Counter" with word-state badges; the chat thread doubles as the contract.
  • The verdict sentence — weather and market screens lead with one plain-language line ("Rain likely from 2PM — spray before noon"; "Maize trading ₦42k–₦48k/bag near you").
  • Drawn 2px-stroke icon set — including agri-specific glyphs (harmattan haze, sacks, sprayer) — replacing the emoji weather icons.
  • Gold merit stamps — best-answer and trusted-trader badges; the only decoration in the system.

Structural decisions fed by research

  • Feed: Nextdoor-style card with place-first context line and four structured payloads (photo, poll, market listing, event); green labeled "Post" FAB; camera-first composer; topic-chip groups with one full-width Join.
  • Marketplace: 2-col photo-is-the-card grid, price-before-title, photographed category tiles, chip-row filters with saved searches; 3-step photo-first sell flow ending in share-to-WhatsApp.
  • Commerce: sticky Make Offer + Buy dual CTA, offer sheet with −5/−10/−15% ₦ chips, delivery/pickup toggle first, pay-on-delivery + escrow row, Bolt-style vertical tracker with "Confirm receipt" releasing escrow.
  • Chat: WhatsApp anatomy with pinned produce card, deal chips (Schedule pickup · Share location · Mark sold), oversized voice notes with transcription, seller quick replies.
  • Utilities: wallet = balance + Add money / Pay seller + running-balance ledger; weather = verdict sentence + gold alert banner + hourly precip rail + advisory chips, pinned to named plots.
  • Structure debt: Profile tab dead-end fixed (settings surface there, not only in a drawer); HomeStack god-stack split per tab; i18n rides the redesign (every new component takes strings from locales).
