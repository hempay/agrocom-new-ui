Agrocom Design Research

Wallet & Weather Utilities

The wallet should behave like a farmer's market cashbox, one huge honest number and two verbs, and weather should behave like a professional work instrument: rain first, real iconography, decisions you can read at arm's length in full sun.

Common patterns

  • Balance → verbs → ledger, in that order. Every wallet home (Wise, GoPay, foodpanda, crypto wallets alike) stacks a large balance, an action row directly beneath it, and a transaction list below. The variable is how many verbs: Wise ships 3, Gojek sprawls to 12.
  • Amount-first entry. Send/pay flows open on a full-screen keypad with a giant amount readout (Coinbase Wallet); recipient and confirmation come after. Mirrors handing over cash before naming the reason.
  • Signed, colored ledger rows grouped by day. Credits in green with a leading +, debits neutral/dark with −, day headers ("Today", "Mon 17 Mar") instead of raw timestamps (GoPay, Setel, WeChat, foodpanda).
  • Status is spelled out, not implied. Mature payment ledgers chip every non-final state: "Pending", "Settled", "Canceled & refunded" (GoPay, Cleo). Users never infer state from color alone.
  • Weather hero = place, one big number, condition word, H/L. Apple Weather, Nextdoor, (Not Boring) all converge on this four-element hero; everything else scrolls beneath it.
  • Hourly rail carries precip % inline. The chance of rain sits directly under each hour's icon, highlighted (blue %) only for wet hours, so a horizontal scan answers "when does it start?" (Apple Weather, Weather Channel).
  • Weekly rows use temp-range bars, not two numbers alone. A low→high gradient bar per day makes the week scannable as a shape (Apple Weather); precip % rides along each row.
  • Alerts are pinned banners above the forecast, never buried. Rain/severe warnings render as a high-contrast pill or banner at the top of the screen ((Not Boring), weatherkit alerts list), tappable to a detail page.

What the best apps do differently

● Wise: verb discipline

Where GoPay and Gojek add icon grids, interest hooks and promo cards, Wise keeps the home to balance + 3 verbs + ledger. Restraint is the differentiator: the screen stays legible and the balance stays the hero.

● WeChat: the running balance

Printing "balance after this transaction" on every row turns the history from a feed into an auditable ledger. No other app in the set does it; it is the single best borrow for a cashbox-honest wallet.

● Weather Channel: sentence-first forecasting

"Outlook: the nicest day in the next 7 days will be Sunday despite scattered storms." A written verdict above the data grid means the decision is made before the user reads a single number, ideal for planning field work.

● AllTrails: weather bent around the activity

It doesn't show weather; it shows trail weather: advisory chips, precip along the route, conditions at the peak. The lesson: contextualize forecast data to the user's task (the farm plot, the spray window), not the city.

● Apple Weather: graded precipitation

Rain is a scale (Light / Moderate / Heavy / Extreme) with a scrubable near-term timeline, not a binary icon. Intensity + timing is exactly what harvest and spraying decisions need.

● (Not Boring): display type as instrument

A poster-sized numeral with tabular data below proves a display face can do the hero work while a workmanlike grid does the detail work, precisely the ClashDisplay + Inter split Agrocom already owns.

Recommendations for Agrocom

Wallet: the market cashbox

● One huge honest number

  • ClashDisplay ₦ balance at ~44–52px on a green #16A34A card, the single hero of the screen, always visible, never behind an eye-toggle by default.
  • Two verbs only, as chunky ≥56px full-width-half buttons directly under the balance: Add money Pay seller. "Withdraw" lives one level down.
  • Pay flow opens amount-first on a big keypad (Coinbase pattern), then recipient, then one confirm screen with fee spelled out in naira.

● A ledger, not a feed

  • Day-grouped rows: counterparty in plain language ("Paid Mama Nkechi, maize, 2 bags"), signed amount, green credits.
  • Print the running balance after each row (WeChat pattern), counts like a cashbox, builds trust.
  • Status chips in words: In escrow Released Refunded. For marketplace orders, an escrow note on the receipt: "Held safely until you confirm delivery."
  • Every payment gets a shareable receipt (image/PDF), traders show receipts, not screens.

Neobank tropes to refuse

  • Icon grids of 8–12 "services" (Gojek): two verbs, full stop.
  • Promo carousels, cashback banners, interest/loan upsells on the wallet home (GoPay).
  • Sparklines, percent-change tickers, portfolio charts (OKX, Uniswap, Glow).
  • Cards/virtual-card skeuomorphism, gradient "premium" cards, points & tiers.
  • Hidden balances by default, abstract hashes/IDs in the ledger, jargon ("liquidity", "yield").

Weather: a work tool, not a wallpaper

● Rain-first hierarchy

  • Lead with the decision: a one-sentence verdict above the data ("Rain likely from 2PM, finish spraying before noon"), Weather Channel outlook-style.
  • Rain alerts as a pinned high-contrast banner under the location, gold #D9A521 for warnings, never a toast.
  • Hourly rail: hour, drawn icon, temp, and precip % highlighted on wet hours; sunrise/sunset as inline events. 7-day rows with low→high range bars + precip % per day.
  • Treat precipitation as a graded scale (light/moderate/heavy) with timing, not a binary cloud icon.

● Section identity & iconography

  • Give weather its own sky-blue identity (#4DA3D9-family surfaces/accents) so it reads as a distinct instrument inside the green app, blue is also the natural color of precip data.
  • Replace all emoji with one drawn icon set (sun, part-cloud, rain, storm, harmattan haze/dust, wind), 2px-stroke, filled variants for active/wet states, legible at 20px and at arm's length.
  • Hero: ClashDisplay temperature + condition word + H/L; Inter tabular numerals for all grid data.

● Planting-relevant advisories

  • Advisory chips computed from conditions (AllTrails pattern): Good day to plant Don't spray: wind 22 km/h Irrigation not needed.
  • Expose the agronomy-relevant fields hidden in "details" elsewhere: wind speed + direction, humidity, dew point, soil-relevant rain totals (mm last 24h / next 24h).
  • Forecast for the user's farm location(s), not just "current city", named saved plots.
Design-language signal: both utilities are instruments, not experiences, one enormous ClashDisplay number (₦ balance / temperature) doing the glanceable work, an Inter tabular grid doing the honest work beneath it, verbs as chunky ≥56px slabs, and words over icons for anything with consequences. High-contrast ink on flat surfaces, alerts as pinned banners, zero decorative gradients: readable through glare, with dusty thumbs, in one glance.
