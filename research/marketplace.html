Agrocom Research

Marketplace: browse & sell

The best classifieds apps treat the photo as the card and the price as the headline: near-zero chrome, price before title, location and recency as trust signals, and a sell flow that starts with the camera and finishes in three taps. Agrocom should copy that skeleton and re-skin it for the open-air produce market: big ₦ figures, per-bag/per-kg units, verified sellers, and photos shot at the stall.

Common patterns

Consistent across Facebook Marketplace, Depop, Mercari, Vinted, eBay, Nextdoor, Karrot and Vestiaire.

01The photo is the card

2-column grid, square-ish photos, hairline or zero borders, no drop shadows. Everything textual lives in a 2–3 line caption below (or overlaid on) the image. Seller-shot, in-context photos outperform studio cutouts on every C2C app.

02Price before title

Facebook writes $65 · 4moms MamaRoo; Nextdoor and eBay bold the price on its own line above the name. The buyer's first question is answered before they read a single word of title.

03Location + recency as the trust line

Local marketplaces put 3w · 0.3 mi · Boston (Nextdoor) or a location chip on the section header (Facebook's "Today's picks · Parsippany"). Distance and freshness substitute for brand trust between strangers.

04Filter chips, not filter pages

One horizontal chip row under search (Filter · Sort · Price · Condition), applied filters rendered as removable chips (Crypto.com, eBay, Vestiaire), full criteria in a bottom sheet. Result counts ("500+ results") confirm the query worked.

05Sell is never more than one tap away

Depop, Vinted and Vestiaire give selling a permanent center tab; Facebook puts a fat "Sell" pill at the very top of Marketplace home. Supply is the lifeblood, so the entrance is unmissable.

06Photo-first listing creation

Every flow starts with the camera / photo picker, not a text form. Photos slots sit at the top of the form (Mercari's 1–20 slots, Nextdoor's cover + drag-to-reorder), and tips push photo quality ("Catch your buyers' eye, use quality photos").

07Drafts, checklists, and completion feedback

"Save to drafts" (Depop, Mercari), green-check checklists with "1 step left" (Vestiaire), and success screens with earnings math + "Sell another item". Listing is treated as a funnel to be protected, never a form to be abandoned.

08Honest price states

Strikethrough for reductions, "Free" as a first-class price, "$60 off" photo badges, fee/profit math shown while pricing (Mercari), all-in buyer price with shield (Vinted). Ambiguity around money is the #1 trust killer, so nobody hides it.

What the best apps do differently

Facebook Marketplace wins on ruthless economy: no card chrome at all, price as the first token of a single caption line, and browse/sell/categories all reachable from one header. It reads like a bulletin board, not a store: which is exactly why it feels local.
Mercari puts the price on the photo itself and shows sellers their fee and net profit while they type the price, the most honest pricing moment in any flow reviewed.
Nextdoor designs for neighbors, not SKUs: fuzzy map circles for pickup location, a Free toggle at parity with price, and recency-plus-distance meta on every card. Its 19-screen flow still feels short because photos come first and the form is one screen.
Vestiaire Collective gamifies completeness, checklist with green ticks, "1 step left", "+30% chance of selling" nudges, converting listing quality into seller motivation instead of a validation error.
Karrot proves category navigation doesn't need taxonomy trees: one grid of photographed objects with two-word labels. Zero-literacy-barrier navigation, directly relevant to Agrocom's audience.

Recommendations for Agrocom

Utilitarian, sunlight-legible, honest about money. NOT fintech: a market square you can carry.

CardsPrice-forward listing cards with big ₦ figures

2-col grid, square produce photo (radius 14, no shadow), then a caption stack led by the price in ClashDisplay at ~20px, the biggest text on the card. Always ₦ (U+20A6), never ASCII "N"; always thousands separators. Meta in Inter ≥ 13px at high contrast.

₦45,000 / bag
Maize, dried: 100kg bag
✓ Verified sellerKaduna · 12 km2h ago

Agri-specificQuantity & unit as a first-class field

Farm trade is priced per unit, not per item. Bake units into the data model and the card: ₦45,000 / bag, ₦1,200 / kg, ₦800 / paint bucket, / crate, / tuber, / 25kg sack. In the sell flow, unit is a chunky chip selector next to the price field, and quantity available ("15 bags left") shows on the listing. This is the single biggest gap in every generic reference app.

TrustLocation + verified-seller chips on every card

Copy Nextdoor's meta line: state/LGA · distance · recency. Add a green-check "Verified" chip tied to Agrocom's WhatsApp/phone verification. On the listing page, show seller name, face, and response time, buyers are choosing a person to haggle with, not a product to checkout.

Sell flowPhoto-first, three steps, ruthless

  • Step 1: Photos: tapping Sell opens the camera immediately (Nextdoor pattern). Up to ~8 photos, first is cover, drag to reorder. Tip banner: "Shoot in daylight, show the heap/sack."
  • Step 2: Details: one screen of chip selectors: category (photo tiles), quantity + unit, condition/grade (fresh / dried / sorted), title auto-suggested from category.
  • Step 3: Price: giant ₦ input with unit suffix, "Negotiable" and "Free" toggles, and a market-price hint ("Maize is trading ₦42k–₦48k/bag near you"). Post or Save draft.

Vestiaire-style progress ("1 step left") and a success screen with "Sell another item" + share-to-WhatsApp.

BrowseCategory tiles with real produce photography

Karrot-style grid, but 2-col and chunky (≥ 100px tall, ≥ 48px tap targets everywhere): Grains, Tubers, Vegetables, Fruits, Livestock, Poultry, Inputs & seeds, Equipment. Photograph real market produce: sacks, crates, heaps, not flat icons. Marketplace home = search bar with camera icon, Sell pill (green, top of screen, Facebook pattern), category rail, then "Near you today" grid with an explicit location chip.

SearchChip-row filters + bottom sheet

Under search: Filters · Sort · Price · Distance · Unit chips with an active-count badge; full sheet holds category, ₦ range slider, distance radius, verified-only toggle. Applied filters render as removable chips above results, with a visible result count. Support "save this search" for repeat buyers (traders restocking weekly).

SunlightLegibility mandates for this flow

White cards on near-white background in-app (the dark board here is report chrome only); text ≥ 13px at ≥ 7:1 contrast for meta, prices near-black; price overlays on photos use a solid scrim, never bare text on image; one green #16A34A for actions and verified marks, gold reserved for price highlights and "featured", never both on the same element.
