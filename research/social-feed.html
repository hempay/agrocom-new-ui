Agrocom Design Research

Social Feed

The best utility communities — Nextdoor, Karrot, Glassdoor — win with light, high-contrast cards, labeled actions, and structured post types (polls, events, items-for-sale), not the dark immersive photo chrome of entertainment feeds. Agrocom's feed should read like a village noticeboard with photography of real produce, not a photography app.

Top examples

Screens examined on Mobbin. Utility-community references first (closest to Agrocom's mandate), then mainstream social anatomy, then composers. Click any card to open on Mobbin.

Nextdoor — home feed
The gold standard for Agrocom. Neighborhood selector in the header, an inline poll with three chunky full-width option buttons, an event card with a bold date block, and a labeled green "Post" FAB (icon + word, not a bare +).
Nextdoor — structured post types
Post category shown inline in the author row ("Alex Smith → For Sale & Free"); the listing itself is an embedded card with photo thumbnail + price. Direct template for Agrocom market/crop-price posts.
Karrot (Daangn) — local group
Real photo cover (food, not illustration), neighborhood + member count + last-active metadata, a gold "group of the month" merit badge, and one full-width high-contrast join CTA. Utilitarian local super-app energy.
Nextdoor — group detail
Purpose-first group page: name + member count + full-width pill Join, then an "About" block stating locality and access ("Anyone can join") before any posts. Trust info precedes content.
Glassdoor — community "Bowl"
Text-first Q&A posts on white, actions spelled out as words ("Like · 5 Comments · Share"), and a sticky full-width green join button. Proof that labeled actions read faster than bare icons for utility audiences.
Lex — hyperlocal feed
Bright green header with a city selector, filter chips (Events / Groups / Friends / Community) directly beneath, and huge display-type post titles. Shows how big type + one loud brand green makes a feed scannable.
Reddit — post detail
Community + author two-line attribution, bold title above a full-width photo, and chunky outlined pill actions (vote / comment / award / share) — tap targets a thumb can hit in a moving vehicle.
Instagram — feed anatomy
Edge-to-edge photo, then like/comment/share/save with counts directly beside icons, then bold-username caption. The canonical photo-post rhythm — but its thin metadata and dense chrome are what Agrocom must scale up.
KakaoBank — reaction chips
A community board inside a utility app: rounded photo, then four large pastel reaction chips (heart / thumbs-up / check / pray) with counts. Reactions-as-buttons, not micro-icons — very sunlight-friendly.
X — composer
Cancel / avatar + "What's happening?" prompt / big rounded photo preview with remove + edit overlays / Add location / reply-audience control / icon toolbar / prominent Post pill. The complete composer checklist in one sheet.
Bluesky — composer sheet
Composer as a modal sheet over the feed: photo dominates, alt-text affordance, "Anybody can interact" chip, character counter. Lightweight, fast, zero-decoration.
Instagram — publish step
Media-first publish screen: thumbnails on top, caption, then option rows (tag, location, audience) and one full-width Share button. Options as list rows, not icon soup.

Common patterns

What repeats across every feed examined, regardless of category.

01Universal post-card anatomy

Avatar (40–44px) + bold author name + a lighter context line (location, group, or timestamp) + overflow "…"; then content (text above media); then an action row. Every app from X to KakaoBank follows this vertical rhythm — deviate and posts stop reading as posts.

02Context line carries place, not clout

Utility feeds replace the follower-count flex with where and when: Nextdoor shows "Lower Allston · 1h", Karrot shows the neighborhood, Reddit shows the community. Identity = person + place + topic.

03Actions sit directly under content, with counts adjacent

Like / comment / share always appear as a single left-aligned row immediately below the media, counts glued to their icons (Instagram, X, Reddit). Glassdoor goes further and labels them with words — the most legible variant.

04Structured post types beat freeform

Nextdoor renders polls as tappable full-width option bars with live results, events as date-block cards, and sale items as photo + price cards embedded inside a normal post shell. The card frame stays constant; only the payload changes.

05Compose = FAB + inline prompt

Two entry points coexist: a bottom-right FAB (X, Bluesky, Lex, Nextdoor) and/or an avatar + "What's happening?" prompt row. Nextdoor's FAB is labeled ("Post" + pencil) — clearer than a bare +.

06Composer sheet checklist

Cancel top-left, disabled→enabled Post pill top-right, avatar + placeholder prompt, large rounded photo preview with an ✕ remove badge, bottom icon toolbar (photo / camera / poll), plus audience & location rows. Identical across X, Bluesky, Weverse, Substack.

07Join is one loud full-width pill

Group pages (Nextdoor, Karrot, Glassdoor) put a single full-width, brand-colored Join button under the cover — often sticky at the viewport bottom. Small inline Join pills only appear in discovery lists (Reddit, Ulta).

08Filters are a horizontal chip rail

Feed scoping lives in one scrollable chip row under the header: Lex (Events / Groups / Friends), Reddit (topic chips), Nextdoor search (Most Recent ▾ / Distance ▾ / All Time ▾). Chips with dropdown carets handle sort; plain chips handle topic.

What the best apps do differently

Nextdoor: the feed is a noticeboard, not a stage

Post types (ask, poll, event, sale, news) are first-class objects with their own embedded card layouts, the neighborhood name anchors the header as a switcher, and the compose FAB says the word "Post". Nothing is optimized for lurking — everything is optimized for getting an answer. This is the single closest model for Agrocom.

Karrot: real photography + trust metadata = local credibility

Group covers are actual phone photos of actual food; every group carries neighborhood, member count, and last-active time; merit badges use a gold accent. It feels like a market square, not a brand. Agrocom's produce photography should work exactly this way — authentic, unfiltered, information-dense.

Glassdoor: words as actions

"Like · 5 Comments · Share" written out in full text — no ambiguity, no icon literacy required. For farmers who may be first-generation smartphone users in glare, labeled actions remove all guesswork.

Lex: one aggressive brand green + display-type headlines makes utility feel designed

Lex proves a utilitarian hyperlocal feed doesn't have to be visually boring: a saturated green header, giant display-font post titles, and chunky chips give it identity without any dashboard widgets — the same trick Agrocom can pull with #16A34A and ClashDisplay.

The counter-examples: VSCO, Lapse, Retro

Dark, full-bleed, chrome-less immersive photo feeds with floating micro-controls. Gorgeous indoors at night — illegible in a field at noon, and they read as art/dating/music apps. This is precisely the aesthetic Agrocom must not adopt, even though it is also "photo-led".

Recommendations for Agrocom

Feed anatomy for sunlight: light surfaces, near-black text, one green, chunky everything.

1

Light card feed, near-black text, 16px+ body

White/warm-off-white cards on a pale neutral canvas (light UIs beat dark in direct sun). Body text ≥16px at near-black (≥ #1A1F1A, 12:1+ contrast); post titles in ClashDisplay 20–22px like Lex; metadata ≥13px at true grey-600, never lighter.

2

Nextdoor-style post card with place-first context line

44px avatar, bold Inter name, context line "Kano · Tomato Growers · 2h" (person + place/group + time), then text, then photo, then actions. Category appears inline in the author row for typed posts: "Adamu → Market Price".

3

Structured post types inside one card shell

Ship four payloads at launch: photo post, question/poll (full-width tappable option bars with live % results), market listing (embedded card: produce photo + crop + price/unit + location), and event/announcement (bold date block). Same frame every time — the farmer's eye learns one card.

4

Produce photography edge-to-edge, 4:3, no filters

Instagram's full-bleed rhythm with Karrot's honesty: photos run edge-to-edge of the card at 4:3 (shows a whole basket/field, taller than 16:9, shorter than square-stacks), rounded 12–14px, no dark gradient overlays hiding content.

5

Labeled, chunky actions — Glassdoor words + Reddit pills

Action row of 48px-tall outlined pills: "👍 Like 24 · 💬 Comment 6 · ↗ Share". Words + counts, minimum 48px tap targets with 8px gaps. Consider one extra "Helpful" reaction (KakaoBank-style chip) — utility feeds reward answers, not applause.

6

Labeled green compose FAB + inline prompt

Bottom-right FAB in #16A34A with pencil + the word "Post" (Nextdoor), plus an avatar + "Share with your community…" prompt row pinned atop the feed. Composer as a sheet: Cancel / green Post pill, big photo preview with ✕, camera-first toolbar (camera before gallery — field usage), audience row (My community / My groups / Everyone).

7

Groups: photo cover, trust metadata, one full-width Join

Group pages lead with a real member photo cover, name in ClashDisplay, "1,240 members · Kaduna · active today", then a single full-width #16A34A Join pill. Discovery lists use small Join pills + topic chips (Cassava, Poultry, Irrigation, Market Prices) in a chip rail under the header.

8

Filter chips + region switcher in the header

Header carries the region/market selector as a dropdown ("Kano ▾", Nextdoor-style) with one scrollable chip rail beneath: All · My Groups · Market Prices · Questions · Events. Chips 40px tall, filled green when active, outlined otherwise.

!

Anti-patterns — hard bans

Thin grey text (X's 12px #6B7280 metadata dies in glare); dark immersive photo chrome (VSCO/Lapse — reads as a music/dating app and is unreadable outdoors); dense dashboard widgets (stat tiles, charts, KPI rows in the feed — fintech smell); icon-only micro-actions under 40px; five greens — every green surface, chip, FAB and Join pill is #16A34A, full stop.

Design-language signal

The noticeboard, not the stage

What separates a rural-first utility feed from urban social apps is where the craft is spent. Urban feeds spend it on immersion — dark chrome, gradients, motion, content bleeding into UI. Nextdoor, Karrot and Glassdoor spend it on declaration: every post declares its type, its place and its ask in the first line; actions are words; buttons are the width of the screen; photography is evidence ("this is my actual harvest") rather than aesthetic. Agrocom's craft move is to make that declarative structure feel designed — ClashDisplay headlines, one confident #16A34A, gold reserved for merit (best answer, trusted trader, group of the month), and honest 4:3 produce photos — so the feed carries the authority of a village noticeboard and the warmth of a market square, and could never be mistaken for a fintech dashboard.
