Chat: list, thread, offers, voice & catalogs

Every strong marketplace chat is WhatsApp with a deal engine bolted on: familiar bubble anatomy underneath, plus a pinned product card, structured offer messages with states and deadlines, and one-tap seller replies layered on top.

Common patterns

What virtually every marketplace-chat app converges on.

01Product pinned at the top of the thread

eBay, Vinted, Grailed, Karrot, Etsy, Airtasker, Vestiaire all pin a compact card, thumbnail, truncated title, price always visible: under the header. It scrolls with nothing; the deal's subject never leaves the screen.

02Offers are structured messages, not text

Offers render as distinct cards/receipts with amount, state (Pending / Declined / Accepted / Expired), and often a countdown ("82hr left"). Vinted shows the original price struck through next to the offer. State changes update the bubble in place.

03Primary deal action pinned near the thumb

Grailed puts a full-width OFFER button above the composer; Vinted puts Make an offer / Buy now in the pinned header; Karrot puts pay/appointment chips under the pin. Negotiating is never buried in a menu.

04System rows narrate the deal

"Seller accepted your offer", "Payment received", "Shipping deadline extended" appear as centered/neutral rows inline. The thread doubles as the transaction's audit trail: you never leave chat to check deal status.

05Trust signals up front

Seller intro cards (Vinted: rating ★, location, last seen), response-time in the header (Karrot "~10 min", Etsy "within 24 hours"), and prominent safety banners ("don't trade outside the app") open nearly every thread.

06WhatsApp bubble grammar is the default

Tinted outgoing bubbles, date pills, in-bubble timestamps, tick-based delivery/read states, pinned-message bar. Deviations (Airtasker's "Read 7 hours ago" text) exist but ticks are the lingua franca.

07Quick replies live above the composer

Taobao's suggestion chips and Karrot's "frequent phrases" in the attachment sheet let sellers answer routine questions ("Is it available?") in one tap. WhatsApp Business does the same with saved replies.

08Voice is a composer-level citizen

WhatsApp, Taobao, LINE, Instagram: mic button directly in/next to the input, hold-to-record with waveform and slide-to-cancel. Taobao adds voice-to-text conversion at release time.

What the best apps do differently

eBay

Treats negotiation as a state machine rendered in chat: offer → counter → expiry, each a card with its own CTA and timer. Old offers persist as history so both parties can see the negotiation arc at a glance.

Karrot

The closest analog to Agrocom's world (local, in-person trade): pin carries the listing status ("Selling"), and the chips under it cover the whole offline deal, schedule a meetup, send payment, add another item. Chat is the deal's control panel.

WhatsApp

Proves rich interactive cards work inside plain bubbles (events, polls with inline "Respond" footers). Its capture UX for voice: full-screen takeover, huge waveform, is the standard the Nigerian market expects.

Taobao

Predictive quick-reply chips adapt to conversation context, and voice notes can be transcribed before sending: a bridge between voice-first users and text-first record keeping.

Airtasker

Pin actions are stage-aware: "Increase price" and "Reschedule" appear only when the job is assigned. The pinned card is dynamic, not a static reference.

Recommendations for Agrocom

WhatsApp-familiar anatomy: the audience lives in WhatsApp, with the trade layered on top. Everything sized for sunlight and thumbs.

  • 1
    Keep WhatsApp's grammar wholesale. Chat list with All / Unread filter chips, big avatars, green unread pills; thread with date pills, in-bubble timestamps, grey/green double ticks; outgoing bubbles in a light green tint of #16A34A. Zero learning curve is the feature.
  • 2
    Pin the produce card at the top of every trade thread (Vinted/Karrot model): photo, "Maize: 50kg bag", price in ClashDisplay, listing status chip (In stock / Sold), plus two chunky actions. Make offer (outline) and Buy / Pay (filled green). Min 48px tap targets, high-contrast text for sunlight.
  • 3
    Structured offer bubbles with inline actions (eBay card × WhatsApp event-card footer):
    Musa offered · ₦45,000/bag × 10
    AcceptCounter

    State badges update in place (Pending → Accepted in green / Declined / Expired in grey), original asking price struck through, optional expiry ("2 days left"). Accepted offers post a system row: "✓ Deal agreed at ₦45,000/bag".
  • 4
    Voice notes as first-class, oversized. Big mic button in the composer, hold-to-record with full-width waveform and slide-to-cancel, playback speed control, and, borrowing Taobao, optional transcription. Many users will negotiate entirely by voice; the play button should be a 44px+ target.
  • 5
    Seller quick replies above the composer. One-tap chips for the trade's routine turns: "Still available", "Last price?", "Send location", "Can deliver". Sellers get editable saved replies (WhatsApp Business style) reachable from the attachment sheet.
  • 6
    Catalog-in-chat. A leaf/basket icon in the composer opens the seller's listings; sharing one drops a full product card bubble (photo, price, unit, "View" action). Taobao's product-in-bubble pattern. This is how a trader quotes multiple crops without leaving the thread.
  • 7
    Narrate the deal with system rows. Offer accepted, payment sent, pickup scheduled, all as centered neutral rows in-thread, so the chat is the receipt. Pair with Karrot-style chips under the pin: Schedule pickup · Share location · Mark sold.
  • ★
    Trust header, sunlight-ready. Thread opens with a seller card: verification badge, rating, market/LGA, "Replies in ~10 min", and the header shows last-seen. Use gold #D9A521 only for ratings and offer-pending accents. Body text ≥16px, bubble text near-black on light bubbles; test every state at max outdoor brightness.

Design-language signal

Trade chat ≠ social chat

Social chat is an open-ended stream where the messages are the point. Trade chat is a negotiation with a subject and an end state: the product stays pinned like a contract header, money moves through structured cards with states and timers instead of prose, and system rows notarize each step until the thread itself is the receipt. For Agrocom that means the UI's job is not to make conversation delightful, it's to make the deal legible at arm's length in full sun: price always on screen, offer state readable in one glance, and the next action (Accept / Counter / Pay) sitting under the thumb as a chunky green button.
