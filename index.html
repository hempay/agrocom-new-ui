Agrocom — Redesign Preview v2 · The Market Square

v2 · research-driven

The Market Square — the authority of a village noticeboard, the warmth of a real market. Screens are light, warm and sunlight-legible (this is the light board of the four projects; only the chrome around the phones is dark): near-black text at 16px and up, one confident green, ₦ figures set in ClashDisplay at display size, photography as evidence, and words wherever a tap has consequences. Rebuilt from the completed Mobbin pass — synthesis at research/index.html. v1 shared a generic card grammar with three sibling boards; v2 is a different architecture: edge-to-edge noticeboard bands instead of floating cards, price-first lockups, chunky labeled pills, drawn 2px icons, zero emoji.

On the photography: every produce, market and face image on this board is licensed stock, used as a stand-in so the preview shows real maize instead of a gradient. Production ships owned or licensed in-situ produce photography — the heap on the mat, the open sack, hands holding the yam, shot in daylight at the stall. Never stock, never picsum (v1's 24 production fallbacks all died).

  • Every ₦ is the real glyph (U+20A6) with thousands separators.
  • Words over icons for consequences — states as word chips, the cost in the button label.
  • ≥48px targets · solid scrims under text-on-photo · place-first context lines everywhere.
  • Not a fintech dashboard. Not dark immersive. Not dating.

07Dark mode — the same screens, one token swap

Agrocom is designed light-first and only light-first: the daytime job is a phone held at arm's length in Kano sun, and that job is won by warm off-white paper, near-black ink and solid scrims — not by a dark theme. Dark mode is therefore a derived mode, not a second design. It serves the other half of the day: evening price-checking after the market closes, chat with a buyer at night, and the battery an OLED screen gives back when most of it is off. The three frames below are the same markup as the Shop home (section 02), the Product page (section 03) and the Wallet (section 05) — copied verbatim, nothing re-laid out, no component forked. A single .dark-scr class on the screen element re-maps the surface, ink, line and tint custom properties and stops there.

What deliberately does not move: the one green #16A34A is byte-identical in both modes — it clears 5.1:1 as text on the dark paper, so every link, "✓ Verified", nav-active label and CTA fill keeps the brand exactly where it is. Ink 16.5:1, secondary 10.8:1, metadata still 7.3:1 — the sunlight contrast floor survives the swap. Photographs are untouched: they carry their own light, so only the scrims strengthen (.82 → .90 alpha) because the images now read brighter against a black surround.
